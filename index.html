My PDF did not read

Some PDFs are pictures, not text. Download the CSV from your online banking instead.

Why only 50% of meals and entertainment?

CRA allows 50%. You also get back only half of the GST/HST on them.

Why 10% home office and 50% vehicle?

They are starting points. Change them on Home to match your office space and your car logbook.

How do I open the Google Sheets file?

Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. It also opens in Excel and Numbers.
